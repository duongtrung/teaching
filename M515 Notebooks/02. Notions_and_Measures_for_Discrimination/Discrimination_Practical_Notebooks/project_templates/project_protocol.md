# Final-project protocol

Case ID (C1, C2, C3 or C4): ____________________

Student/team: ____________________

1. **Question and contribution:** One falsifiable question; explain what goes beyond the guided lesson.
2. **Decision and affected people:** What action is being audited? Is a positive decision favorable or adverse? Who chose the objective?
3. **Data and unit:** Original source, snapshot, sample scope, independent unit, repeated conditions and availability limitations.
4. **Hypothesis:** Direction, comparison/reference, primary metric, practical effect-size threshold and unit.
5. **Label and score validity:** What is observed? What is unobserved? Which labels or scores may reflect historical or annotation bias?
6. **Intervention and baseline:** How will the comparison answer the question? What is held fixed? What changes?
7. **Validation:** Split/resampling unit, leakage prevention, tuning plan, final holdout and treatment of exploratory analyses.
8. **Uncertainty/sensitivity:** Interval/test assumptions, small groups, multiple comparisons, missingness and failure policies.
9. **Ethical response:** Privacy, consent, coverage, proxy use, annotator work, harm distribution, explanation and recourse.
10. **Deliverables and responsibility:** Who implements, reviews and interprets each element? What evidence would change the recommendation?

Record this protocol before inspecting new comparisons. Date and describe later changes.
