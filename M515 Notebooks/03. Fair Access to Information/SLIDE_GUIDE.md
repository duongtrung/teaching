Every student submits one figure, one checked calculation and a 150-word ethical decision memo. The memo must identify an affected person or group, the opportunity at stake, a guiding principle, a feasible remedy with owner and cost, and an evidence gap. A correct gap calculation alone does not complete the assignment.
